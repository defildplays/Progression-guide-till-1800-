START HERE1640 / 1660

Get to 1660, then let the events carry you to 1700

This guide starts when you receive your Tier 4 gear from North Kurzan or enter at 1660 with the South Rimeria II Powerpass.

01

Story route: 1640 → 1660

Finish North Kurzan and The Way Forward. Equip the Tier 4 Determination of Destiny +10 set for 1640. Turn on Ark Passive, then Advanced Hone all six pieces to AH 20 to reach 1660.

02

Powerpass route: start at 1660

Use a South Rimeria II Powerpass if your account has one. It places the character at 1660, so you can skip the 1640 → 1660 honing step.

03

At 1660: select both events

Put the same character into Mokoko Base Camp: Jump-Up Boost and Rapid Growth. Do this before buying progression items.

Before opening selectors: choose one class build and stick to it. Use that same build for skills, combat stats, engravings, Ark Passive, gems and Order Cores.Open Lost Ark Nexus →

Jump-Up Boost + Rapid Growth

Do not manually hone 1660 → 1700. Complete the Jump-Up Warm-Up Program and equip the pre-enhanced milestone gear. Both Jump-Up and Rapid Growth keep giving progression rewards through 1720.

Do this in order
1

Copy one complete class build

Set the exact skills, tripods, runes, combat stats, engravings, Ark Passive and gem assignments from one current class guide. Do not mix two builds.

2

Claim all free progression first

Claim Jump-Up, Rapid Growth and event-shop rewards before replacing gems, accessories, bracelet, Cores or Astrogems.

3

Turn on Ark Grid

Equip the three class-specific Order Cores and three general Chaos Cores your build wants. Socket Astrogems until the needed Core effects activate.

4

AH 20 → 30 on all six pieces

Leave normal honing at +18. Advanced Hone every armor piece and the weapon from AH 20 to AH 30. All six at AH 30 = 1710.

Ark Grid: Order Cores are class-specific. Chaos Cores are shared effects. Core Points activate the Core effect; Willpower limits which Astrogems fit. Karma 6/21 means Rank 6, Level 21. 7/7 stone means seven successful facets on both useful engraving lines.
Damage gems
Main damage skill Lv. 8; other damage skills Lv. 7. Put extra event Lv. 8 gems on the next highest-damage skills.
Cooldown gems
Lv. 7 on every cooldown skill your rotation uses.
Support gems
Prioritize cooldown on brand, identity/buff, shield and utility skills. Use the exact assignments from your support build.
Support accessories
For useful refinement effects, prioritize Ally Atk. Power, then Ally Damage, then Brand Power.
Engravings
Use all 5 correct combat engravings at Legendary. Do not buy random Relic books yet.
Ability Stone
Use a stone with the two correct engravings. 7/7 is a strong practical target; 9/7 is not needed.
Accessories
Keep good event accessories. If you buy a piece, one strong useful refinement effect is enough here.
Bracelet
Use the two combat stats your class wants + at least 1 useful damage effect.
