START HERE0 → 1700

Story or Powerpass → 1700

Choose one complete class build first, then use the current event path to reach 1700 without following an outdated Tier 3 shopping list.

01

Choose your class

Pick a class and one build before opening class-specific selectors. Keep skills, engravings, Ark Passive and Order Cores aligned to the same build.

02

Reach Tier 4

Following the story through North Kurzan gives 1640 Tier 4 gear. From there, Advanced Hone all six gear pieces to +20 to reach 1660. Alternatively, a South Rimeria II Powerpass can start an eligible character directly at 1660.

03

Mokoko Base Camp

At 1660+, select your character for Jump-Up Boost. Follow the Warm-Up Program and equip the pre-enhanced milestone gear at 1670, 1680, 1690 and 1700—do not manually hone through those steps.

04

Rapid Growth

Select the intended character while it is 1660–1700. Claim Rapid Growth rewards at 1660, 1700, 1705, 1710, 1715 and 1720 before buying the same progression items yourself.

Current fast-track: Jump-Up Boost + Rapid Growth

Use both active progression systems before spending Gold. Jump-Up handles the 1660 → 1700 gear climb with milestone gear; Rapid Growth keeps paying out through 1720.

Eligible when selected at 1660–1700. Official milestone rewards include a Rapid Growth Gear Chest, growth materials and Order Astrogem chests through 1720.

KEEP MOVINGROUTINE

Your daily and weekly routine

You do not need a roster full of alts to follow this guide. Start with one character, learn its rotation, and use the content that feeds the next upgrade.

Daily / available entries

Kurzan Front: current Tier 4 materials and eligible Astrogem rewards.

At 1730, Chaos Rift: switch to upper Tier 4 materials; it shares Aura of Resonance with Kurzan Front.

Guardian Raid: clear an eligible Guardian and collect the available soul reward.

Weekly progression

Gold raids: clear the raids you can do reliably and learn the next one with a learning group.

Serca: collect materials for the 1730 transfer and later Breakthrough.

Horizon Cathedral: use the highest stage you can access for Cores and Graced Shards.

1730+ extras

Haal's Hourglass: use collected Sand of Trial; up to five can improve that week's reward.

Paradise: progress the current season and equip the useful Orb you have earned.

Shops & missions: claim weekly exchanges and event rewards before buying materials.

Roster power between raids

Follow the class guide for Skill Point Potions and runes. Equip the correct awakened card set, pet effect and Ark Passive points. Practice the rotation in Trixion before learning a raid.
