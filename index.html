Story or Powerpass → 1700

Choose one complete class build first, then use the current event path to reach 1700 without following an outdated Tier 3 shopping list.

01

Choose your class

Pick a class and one build before opening class-specific selectors. Keep skills, engravings, Ark Passive and Order Cores aligned to the same build.

02

Reach Tier 4

Following the story through North Kurzan gives 1640 Tier 4 gear. A South Rimeria II Powerpass can start an eligible character at 1660.

03

Mokoko Base Camp

Select the intended character for Jump-Up Boost! at 1660+. Equip the pre-enhanced gear from the 1670, 1680, 1690 and 1700 milestones.

04

Rapid Growth

Use Rapid Growth on the eligible event character and claim milestones through 1720. Check selection rules before progressing beyond an event's eligibility window.

At 1700: equip all six +18 Destined Hellfire pieces with Advanced Honing 20. Normal honing level and Advanced Honing are separate values.

What the damage gain means

0% → 3%GRADE BASE ATTACK POWER BONUS

Epic has no grade Base Attack Power bonus. Legendary is +1%, Relic +2%, and Ancient +3%. Honing also raises the Bracer's raw stats and Combat Power.

Do not read +1,420 CP as +18.9% DPS. Combat Power and real raid damage are not the same thing. The CP table is a progression estimate; the exact damage gain depends on your complete build.

The week markers are a Korean progression estimate. Extra materials from western solo shops, events or other sources can shorten the timeline.

DPS bracelet rolls: what to keep

Start with the two combat stats your build uses

Crit, Specialization and Swiftness are not interchangeable. Keep the pair your class guide asks for, then judge the extra lines. A bracelet with the correct two stats plus one good damage line is already a strong progression piece.

~0.15%ROUGH DAMAGE VALUE PER 1,350 MAIN STAT
Main stat is a smaller line than the best special effects, but it is still real damage and should be included when comparing two complete bracelets.

High-value offensive rolls

CirculateFervorHammerPreciseSuperiorityAssailAmbushBattleWedge + Hammer

Ambush is only for Back Attack builds; Battle is only for Frontal Attack builds. Precise loses value near crit cap, while Hammer changes value with your existing Crit Damage. Wedge is much more attractive when paired with Hammer.

Compare the whole bracelet, not one line. Two correct combat stats + a useful effect can beat a bracelet with a flashy special roll but weak stats. For close comparisons, use your class's real Crit Rate, Crit Damage and stat breakpoints.
KEEP MOVINGROUTINE

Your daily and weekly routine

You do not need a roster full of alts to follow this guide. Start with one character, learn its rotation, and use the content that feeds the next upgrade.

Daily / available entries

Kurzan Front: current Tier 4 materials and eligible Astrogem rewards.

At 1730, Chaos Rift: switch to upper Tier 4 materials; it shares Aura of Resonance with Kurzan Front.

Guardian Raid: clear an eligible Guardian and collect the available soul reward.

Weekly progression

Gold raids: clear the raids you can do reliably and learn the next one with a learning group.

Serca: collect materials for the 1730 transfer and later Breakthrough.

Horizon Cathedral: use the highest stage you can access for Cores and Graced Shards.

1730+ extras

Haal's Hourglass: use collected Sand of Trial; up to five can improve that week's reward.

Paradise: progress the current season and equip the useful Orb you have earned.

Shops & missions: claim weekly exchanges and event rewards before buying materials.

Roster power between raids

Follow the class guide for Skill Point Potions and runes. Equip the correct awakened card set, pet effect and Ark Passive points. Practice the rotation in Trixion before learning a raid.
